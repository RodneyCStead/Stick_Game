# Stick It To Em - Art Asset Guide

## Required Art Assets for AI Generation

### **Character Sprites** (60x120 pixels recommended)
Place in: `assets/sprites/characters/`

**File Names Needed:**
- `player.png` - Main stick figure character
- `goblin.png` - Basic enemy stick figure
- `orc.png` - Medium enemy stick figure  
- `troll.png` - Large enemy stick figure

**AI Prompts:**
- "Simple black stick figure character, clean lines, game sprite, white background, 60x120 pixels"
- "Stick figure enemy with small weapon, minimal design, black lines on white background"
- "Larger stick figure with club, enemy character, simple design, game art"

### **Card Art** (120x160 pixels recommended)  
Place in: `assets/sprites/cards/`

**File Names Needed:**
- `strike.png` - Basic attack
- `heavy_strike.png` - Powerful attack
- `heal.png` - Healing ability
- `power_strike.png` - Ultimate attack

**AI Prompts:**
- "Stick figure punching, action pose, card game art, 120x160 pixels"
- "Stick figure with weapon raised, heavy attack, simple black lines"
- "Stick figure with green healing aura, hearts around character"
- "Stick figure with energy effect, power attack, dramatic pose"

### **Platform/Background** (1000x200 pixels recommended)
Place in: `assets/sprites/platform/`

**File Names Needed:**
- `battlefield.png` - Main battle arena

**AI Prompts:**
- "Simple platform or ground for stick figures to stand on, minimal design, game background"
- "Battle arena background, clean and simple, suitable for stick figure game"

### **UI Elements** (Various sizes)
Place in: `assets/sprites/ui/`

**Optional Files:**
- `bottom_panel.png` - UI background
- `card_frame.png` - Card border design
- `health_bar.png` - Custom health bar

## Current Fallbacks
The game will work without art assets by using:
- Colored rectangles for characters
- Simple colored cards
- Basic UI elements

Once you add the PNG files with the correct names, the game will automatically use them!

## Tips for AI Art Generation
1. Keep stick figures simple with clean black lines
2. Use white or transparent backgrounds
3. Make sure characters are clearly visible
4. Action poses should be easy to understand
5. Keep the art style consistent across all assets
