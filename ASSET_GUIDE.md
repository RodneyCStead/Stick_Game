# Stick It To Em - Art Asset Guide

## Required Art Assets for AI Generation

### **Character Sprites** (60x120 pixels recommended)
Place in: `assets/sprites/characters/`

**File Names Needed:**
- `player.png` - Main stick figure character
- `goblin.png` - Basic enemy stick figure
- `orc.png` - Medium enemy stick figure  
- `troll.png` - Large enemy stick figure


### **Card Art** (120x160 pixels recommended)  
Place in: `assets/sprites/cards/`

**File Names Needed:**
- `slash.png` - Sword attack (8 damage)
- `kick.png` - Physical attack (6 damage)
- `healing_potion.png` - Healing utility (10 health)
- `shields_up.png` - Defense enhancement (8 armor)

### **Platform/Background** (1000x200 pixels recommended)
Place in: `assets/sprites/platform/`

**File Names Needed:**
- `battlefield.png` - Main battle arena

**AI Prompts:**
- "Simple platform or ground for stick figures to stand on, minimal design, game background"
- "Battle arena background, clean and simple, suitable for stick figure game"

### **UI Elements** (Various sizes)
Place in: `assets/sprites/ui/`
